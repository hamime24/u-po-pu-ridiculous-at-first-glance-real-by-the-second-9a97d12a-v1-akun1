# Ridiculous at First Glance, Real by the Second — Entry

One sentence, at most 200 characters, pitching a bizarre startup
idea that sounds absurd until you realise it would actually sell.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the pitch to `/output/entry.txt`.
Stdlib only, offline, deterministic.

## The pitch

> A subscription service that rents out professional apologizers to write your breakup texts, sorry notes, and HR complaints — because nobody likes saying it themselves.

167 characters, one sentence.
